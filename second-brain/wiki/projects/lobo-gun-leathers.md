# Lobo Gun Leathers — lead (EN, odmietol)

US kožiar — holstery, 54 rokov v biznise. Tier 2 z Cart Leads CSV ([[cold-outreach-pipeline]]). Otvárač z [[outreach-batch-5]].

## Stav konverzácie

**Reagovala (k 17.7., zbadané v inboxe 20.7.):** objednávky idú výhradne cez telefón/email kvôli veľkému počtu variácií produktov, odhadovaný **16-týždňový** čas dodania. Silný signál operatívneho preťaženia — presne ICP (dopyt existuje, kanál je manuálny bottleneck). Marko odoslal 20.7. reveal + pitch na guided order form.

**Odpovedal znova (k 21.7., zbadané v inboxe 24.7.):** obhajuje telefón ako hodnotu, nie ako bottleneck — pri hovore vie zákazníka viac do hĺbky prevedieť rôznymi modelmi, poradiť podľa štýlu nosenia a typu postavy, a najmä prví zákazníci tak pochopia, ako sa dizajny líšia. Vecná námietka, nie odmietnutie. **Marko odpovedal 24.7.** reframe: guided form nenahrádza konzultáciu, len pred-filtruje, aby telefónny čas išiel na vážnych kupcov namiesto opakovaných úvodných otázok. Otázka späť: koľko z prvého hovoru padne na základy vs. koľko ľudí príde už pripravených.

**Odpovedal na reframe (24.7., zbadané v inboxe 3.8.):** vysvetlil, že väčšina ľudí vie, čo chce, ale prvokupci potrebujú konzultáciu k výberu podľa spôsobu nosenia — obhajuje telefón do hĺbky. Marko sa spýtal priamo: „Do you need an eshop?" (dátum neistý, medzi 24.7.–3.8.). **Odpoveď (zbadaná v inboxe 3.8., ~6 dní staré, teda cca 28.7.): „No we are good."** Definitívne, zdvorilé odmietnutie. **Stav: done.**

## Komunikácia (IG DM)

**Marko (otvárač, 16.7.):**
> Hey! 54 years making holsters is a serious run — that kind of longevity usually means a real waitlist. Curious how orders actually work day to day: mostly phone/email back and forth, or can people order straight off lobogunleather.com?

**Lobo Gun Leathers (k 17.7., prijaté v inboxe 20.7.):**
> We prefer to do the orders via phone and email just because there are so many different variations even with in the variations of the different models. We are guessing a 16 week turn around at this time too.

**Marko (20.7., cez Kimi WebBridge):**
> Makes sense with that many variations — phone/email probably beats a confusing dropdown menu. That said, 16 weeks is a long wait when it starts as an unstructured call. I build stores for makers, and a guided order form (walks someone through the exact variation options, captures everything up front before it hits your phone) could cut a lot of that back-and-forth without forcing anyone through a generic checkout. Worth a look, or does phone/email genuinely work fine for you as-is?

**Lobo Gun Leathers (k 21.7., prijaté v inboxe 24.7.):**
> Phone works very well just because we can talk more in depth about the different holsters and where they excel and get you into a holster that will work in the application that you want. We get first time people and this helps them understand how the different designs work and apply to their style of carry and body type

**Marko (24.7., cez Kimi WebBridge):**
> That makes sense, the consult is the value and a dropdown can't replace it. I wasn't picturing a checkout though. More a short form that captures model, carry style and body type before the call, so the phone time goes to serious buyers instead of the same first questions. Does most of that first call go on basics, or are people usually ready by the time they reach you?

**Lobo Gun Leathers (24.7., 23:52):**
> Most people know what they want when they contact us but sometimes we have people that its their first time getting a holster for a gun and don't know what they need for their application and thats when we work through the small finer details with them and why we suggest certain holsters and options on said holster

**Marko (dátum neistý, medzi 24.7.–3.8.):**
> Do you need an eshop?

**Lobo Gun Leathers (~28.7., zbadané v inboxe 3.8.):**
> No we are good
