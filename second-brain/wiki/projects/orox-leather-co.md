# Orox Leather Co. — lead (EN, reagoval pozitívne)

US kožiar, Portland OR, 18K IG. **4. generácia mexicko-americkej kožiarskej rodiny od 1933** — overený,
nevymyslený príbeh. Shopify store, redesign-grade (kompetentný, ale bez rodinnej histórie na stránke).
Brand-first otvárač z [[outreach-batch-8]] (lead #19).

## Stav konverzácie

**Oslovený 20.7.** — hook: štyri generácie remesla sa nikde na webe nespomínajú, hoci je to najsilnejší
možný príbeh značky.

**Reagoval (25.7., zbadané v inboxe 3.8.):** „We are a small team, we only have it showing on our about
page but thank you we should integrate it more to each page specially the front" — **potvrdil pain point
bez obrany**, malý tím, súhlasí, že príbeh treba dostať viac dopredu (hlavne na homepage). Otvorená,
neobranná reakcia — dobrý signál na pokračovanie pitchom.

## Komunikácia (IG DM)

**Marko (otvárač, 20.7.):**
> Hey! Four generations since 1933 is the kind of story most heritage brands invent — you actually have it. That's what makes me curious about the site: it's a competent Shopify store, but nothing about it tells me I'm buying from a family that's been doing this for 90 years. Does that story show up anywhere people actually see it?

**Orox Leather Co. (25.7., zbadané v inboxe 3.8.):**
> We are a small team, we only have it showing on our about page but thank you we should integrate it more to each page specially the front

**Marko (fáza 2, 3.8., cez Kimi WebBridge):**
> That's the move, especially the homepage since that's what most people see first. I build stores for makers, cart.design. Happy to sketch out where the family story could go if that's useful. Want me to take a quick look?
